# Vegetal Hide

No more animal killing! This mods gives you a fair vegetal alternative to get hide.

## Craft

### Workbench > Tinkering
- **Light Hide** : 2 Tree Sap, 2 Plant Fiber, 2 Brown Common Mushroom
- **Medium Hide** : 4 Tree Sap, 4 Plant Fiber, 4 Brown Cap Mushroom
- **Heavy Hide** : 6 Tree Sap, 6 Plant Fiber, 6 White Cap Mushroom
